## 5. Zero Server Retention & Local Data Ownership

In accordance with our Privacy Policy:
- **No Cloud Database Storage**: ZingTalk operates on a Zero Server Storage architecture. Text messages are never written to any persistent server database or log. All message history and contact lists reside **exclusively on the user's local device** (`localStorage` / local device preferences).
- **Contacts**: Contact directories are private to your physical device. When you log out or clear app cache, all local contacts and chat records are permanently erased.
- **Peer-to-Peer Calls**: Video and audio sessions flow directly peer-to-peer via encrypted WebSockets and WebRTC DTLS-SRTP pipelines without passing through recording or media archiving servers.
