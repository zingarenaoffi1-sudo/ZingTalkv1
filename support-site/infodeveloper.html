Split-Mesh Architecture

How ZingTalk TV Connects with ZingTalk Connector

Instead of overloading low-RAM Fire TV Sticks with dual HD video streams, ZingTalk splits upload and download paths automatically.

Option 2 Split-Mesh Media & Control Pipeline

Every TV call stays smooth even on entry-level Fire TV Sticks because heavy camera encoding runs on your phone hardware while your TV handles big-screen decoding.

1. Direct Camera & Mic Uplink

Connector Phone → Friend

Your paired Android phone captures HD camera video + Echo-Guarded mic audio and streams directly via WebRTC to the remote friend.

0% Fire TV Encode Load
2. Big-Screen HD Downlink

Friend → ZingTalk TV

The remote caller's video and voice play smoothly on your Fire TV display and living-room speakers with ultra-low latency.

1080p Leanback Playback
3. Couch Control Bridge

Connector ↔ TV Control

Every D-Pad press, answer/hang-up action, and virtual keyboard keystroke travels over TLS-encrypted Cloud WSS to navigate ZingTalk TV.

6-Digit / QR Authenticated
1

Open & Sign In on TV

Launch ZingTalk TV on your Fire TV or Android TV and sign in to activate your 10-digit UID.

2

Open Phone Cam Modal

Using your TV remote, select the Phone Cam button in the header to display your 6-digit pairing code and QR code.

3

Scan QR or Enter Code

Open ZingTalk Connector on your phone, tap Scan QR or enter the TV UID + 6-digit code, then tap Connect.

4

Call, Switch Cams & Type

Once TV shows Phone Cam Ready, start any call. Use Connector to preview/flip front-rear cameras or control TV menus with the virtual keyboard.

Pro Tip — Echo Guard Acoustic Separation: Place your paired Connector phone on a coffee table or tripod slightly in front of you facing away from the TV speakers, at moderate TV volume. ZingTalk Connector engages an automatic 90Hz high-pass filter, AEC echo cancellation, and noise suppression so your friend never hears TV echo.
Core Engineering

Built for Real-Time Reliability & Zero-Storage Privacy

Enterprise WebRTC transport, ephemeral TURN relays, and hardened Android WebView security across every release.

DTLS-SRTP Encryption

All peer-to-peer voice and video media is encrypted end-to-end with 256-bit DTLS-SRTP. ZingTalk servers never record, listen to, or archive your conversations.

Ephemeral Signaling & TURN

Session handshakes and HMAC time-limited NAT relay credentials exist only during active sessions, keeping strict firewall and mobile CGNAT calls rock-solid.

Android Hardware Acceleration

Leanback TV layouts, hardware H.264/VP8 codec acceleration, automatic audio fallback, and strict 18+ compliance with household parental control guidance.

Hardware Permissions Across All 3 Apps

ZingTalk requests runtime Android / Fire OS permissions strictly when needed:

  • CAMERA: Used on Mobile and TV (with USB webcam) during video calls, and on ZingTalk Connector exclusively for QR pairing scan & live Split-Mesh uplink when connected.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Captures live microphone audio during active calls and Connector Split-Mesh sessions with Echo Guard DSP active.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS & USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT: Delivers incoming call ring alerts on ZingTalk Mobile.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_PHONE_CALL / MICROPHONE & WAKE_LOCK: Prevents Android from dropping active calls or paired Connector camera streams when minimized.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS & INTERNET: Handles WebRTC signaling, Echo Guard routing, and Bluetooth/speaker switching.
