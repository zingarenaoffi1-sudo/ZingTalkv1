Strict Age Restriction (18+) & Global Minor Privacy Compliance

ZingTalk is strictly intended for adults aged 18 and older. To ensure complete compliance with global data protection statutes, digital age-of-majority regulations, and international child/minor online safety frameworks — including the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) (15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), the European Union & UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR Article 8), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023), and Brazil's LGPD — ZingTalk DOES NOT permit any individual under 18 years of age (minors, teenagers, or children) to register, log in, or use this application.

  • Zero Minor Data Collection: We do not knowingly collect, request, solicit, maintain, or process personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 18. Our services are neither directed at nor designed to appeal to children or minors under any global jurisdiction.
  • Third-Party Advertising & App Store Compliance: Because ZingTalk integrates third-party commercial advertising networks to fund free server infrastructure, users under the age of 18 are strictly restricted from accessing the service in accordance with global advertising standards and platform policies (including Amazon Appstore and Google Play Store child-directed restrictions).
  • Parental Controls & Household Devices: For shared household devices such as Smart TVs and Fire TV, legal guardians and adult device owners are solely responsible for enabling system-level Parental Controls and PIN restrictions to prevent unauthorized access by minors under 18.
  • Immediate Account Termination: If we discover or are notified that an account belongs to an individual under the age of 18, we will immediately and permanently delete the account, terminate active session tokens, and erase all related records from our database.
  • Parental Deletion Requests: Parents or legal guardians who believe a minor under 18 has bypassed age restrictions and accessed ZingTalk may contact us directly at support.zing.app@gmail.com. Upon verification, we will permanently purge the account within 24 hours.

Advertisements & Third-Party Ad Networks Disclosure

ZingTalk uses Third-Party Advertising to keep voice calls, HD video calls, Split-Mesh TV Phone Cam pairing, and instant messaging free for all adult users worldwide.

To support high-bandwidth real-time media relay infrastructure and continuous application maintenance, ZingTalk displays commercial advertisements (including banner ads, interstitial full-screen ads, and native promotional units) provided by certified third-party advertising partners and mobile/TV ad networks.

  • Adult Audience Ad Compliance (18+): Because ZingTalk is strictly restricted to users aged 18 and older, advertisements are served exclusively to adult audiences in full compliance with international privacy laws (COPPA, GDPR, CCPA, DPDP Act) and store publisher guidelines.
  • Data Collected by Ad SDKs: Third-party ad networks may automatically receive technical device identifiers, such as advertising identifiers (GAID / Fire Ad ID), approximate location (country/city level from IP, never precise GPS), device model, and OS version to deliver relevant advertisements, measure campaign performance, and detect click fraud.

    • Smart TV / Fire TV: Settings → Preferences → Privacy Settings → Advertising ID → Interest-Based Ads [OFF].

2. Zero Server Storage & Split-Mesh Calling Privacy

ZingTalk is engineered around zero-knowledge call media handling:

  • Zero Call Audio & Video Recording: All voice, HD video, and ZingTalk Connector Phone Cam calls run over direct WebRTC peer-to-peer pipelines protected by 256-bit DTLS-SRTP encryption. ZingTalk servers NEVER record, listen to, intercept, analyze, or archive audio conversations or video streams.
  • Option 2 Split-Mesh Uplink Privacy: When pairing ZingTalk Connector with ZingTalk TV, your phone streams encrypted WebRTC camera/microphone media directly to your call partner while your TV receives the partner's video. Connector pairing uses volatile 6-digit codes / QR payloads that expire immediately when disconnected.
  • Ephemeral Signaling & Remote Control Bridge: Signaling relays exchange temporary SDP offers, ICE candidates, and live Connector D-Pad/keyboard events purely in volatile RAM during active sessions and discard them on disconnect.
  • Zero Cloud Message Archiving: Instant text messages flow directly between online participants. If offline, encrypted message packets wait in volatile server memory only until delivery confirmation.
  • 100% Local Contacts Storage: Saved contacts remain strictly inside local device storage (localStorage). ZingTalk never uploads or sells your address book.

4. Android & Fire OS Permissions Disclosure

Here is the exact reason each hardware permission is requested across ZingTalk TV, Mobile, and Connector:

  • CAMERA: Captures live video during calls on Mobile/TV, scans TV QR pairing codes inside ZingTalk Connector, and streams wireless Split-Mesh Phone Cam video when paired. Camera access stops immediately when preview/call ends.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Captures live microphone audio during calls and Connector Split-Mesh sessions (with Echo Guard 90Hz DSP active). Audio is never recorded to storage.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Displays incoming voice and video call banners and ongoing call controls on Mobile.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_PHONE_CALL & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE (Android 14+): Keeps active calls and paired Connector camera/mic uplinks alive when the screen turns off or apps switch.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT: Shows high-priority incoming call screens over the lock screen on ZingTalk Mobile.
  • VIBRATE & WAKE_LOCK: Provides ring alerts and keeps the display awake during active video calls.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS & INTERNET: Routes audio between earpiece, speaker, and Bluetooth devices and connects to TLS/WSS signaling & WebRTC media relays.
