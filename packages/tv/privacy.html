2. Zero Server Storage & Calling Privacy

ZingTalk is architected from the ground up around strict privacy principles:

  • Zero Call Audio & Video Recording: All one-on-one voice and video calls operate over direct WebRTC (Web Real-Time Communication) peer-to-peer pipelines protected by DTLS (Datagram Transport Layer Security) and SRTP (Secure Real-Time Transport Protocol) 256-bit encryption. ZingTalk servers NEVER record, listen to, intercept, analyze, or archive audio conversations or video streams.
  • Ephemeral Signaling: Signaling servers are used solely to exchange initial session handshakes (SDP offers/answers and ICE candidate network routing). Once the callers are connected directly, media flows strictly device-to-device. Handshake signaling metadata is immediately discarded from memory.
  • Zero Cloud Message Archiving: Instant text messages and media attachments flow directly between online participants. Messages are not written to permanent cloud databases or log disks. If a recipient is temporarily offline, encrypted message packets are held in volatile server RAM solely until delivery and are permanently deleted the millisecond delivery is confirmed.
  • 100% Local Contacts Storage: Saved contacts and address book shortcuts reside strictly inside your local device storage (localStorage). ZingTalk never uploads, synchronizes, sells, or shares your personal contacts list to cloud databases.

4. Android & TV Permissions Disclosure

In compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies and Appstore Guidelines, here is the transparent breakdown of device permissions ZingTalk requests and why each is required:

  • RECORD_AUDIO: Essential to capture microphone audio during real-time voice and video calls. Microphone audio is processed live and transmitted directly to your call partner; it is never stored.
  • CAMERA: Essential to capture camera video for two-way HD video calling. Video is streamed live peer-to-peer and is never saved.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+ / API 33+): Used exclusively to alert you of incoming voice and video calls (heads-up banner) and show persistent active call status in the notification shade.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_PHONE_CALL & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE (Android 14+ / API 34+): Mandated by modern Android OS to prevent the operating system from terminating active voice/video calls when you minimize the app, switch apps, or lock your screen.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT: Permits ZingTalk to display the high-priority incoming call notification over the lock screen so you never miss an incoming call.
  • VIBRATE & WAKE_LOCK: Vibrates the device during incoming call ringing and keeps the screen active while you are actively speaking on a call.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Routes call audio between phone earpiece, loudspeaker, and connected Bluetooth headsets.
