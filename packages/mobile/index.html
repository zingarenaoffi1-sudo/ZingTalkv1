🔒 Privacy Policy & Terms

⚠️ COPPA Rule & Strict Age 13+: ZingTalk is strictly for individuals aged 13 and older. Children under 13 are prohibited from registering or using ZingTalk. We do not collect data from children under 13.
📢 Advertisements (Ads): ZingTalk displays third-party ads via certified advertising networks to keep voice/video calls and chat 100% free for everyone.

Zero Server Storage: Audio and video calls are direct peer-to-peer (WebRTC DTLS-SRTP encrypted). Calls are NEVER recorded or listened to.

Local Contacts: Contacts reside strictly in your local device memory (localStorage) and are never stored on any cloud database.

Anonymous 10-Digit UID: No phone numbers or SIM cards needed.

Support & Deletion: Email support.zing.app@gmail.com for any inquiry or account deletion requests.
