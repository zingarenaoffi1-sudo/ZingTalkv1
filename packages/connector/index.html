How to Use ZingTalk Connector with Fire TV

Turn any Android smartphone into a wireless HD webcam, microphone, and touch remote for ZingTalk TV — without buying a USB webcam.

01

How Split-Mesh Calling Works

Unlike slow screen-mirroring, ZingTalk runs two direct paths in parallel:

  • Phone Camera to Friend: Your phone's camera and mic stream 720p/1080p directly to your friend via WebRTC.
  • Friend Video to Fire TV: Your friend's video goes straight to your television screen in HD.
  • Why it feels smooth: Your Fire TV Stick only decodes incoming video and never has to encode camera video, preventing heat and stutter.
02

Step-by-Step Pairing

  1. Open ZingTalk TV on your Amazon Fire TV and sign in (Email or Guest Mode).
  2. Select Phone Cam on the TV top bar to open the 6-digit Pairing Code & QR screen.
  3. Open ZingTalk Connector on this phone, go to Pair TV, type the 6-digit code (or tap Scan TV QR), and tap Connect to Fire TV.
  4. Your TV top bar updates to Phone Cam Ready. Keep ZingTalk Connector open while calling.
03

Best Phone Placement & Zero Echo Tips

  • Place your phone upright near the TV (below or beside the screen) on a stand or tripod, 3 to 6 feet from the sofa.
  • Keep TV speaker volume at a comfortable medium level (avoid max TV volume facing the phone mic).
  • Keep Echo Guard & 90Hz Filter enabled in the Cam & Mic tab to cut TV cabinet bass resonance.
  • Both Wi-Fi and mobile data (4G/5G) work anywhere thanks to TURN relay support.
04

Remote Control & Troubleshooting

  • Start or Answer Calls: Use the TV remote or the TV Remote tab here — your phone camera uplink starts automatically the second the call connects.
  • Fast Typing: Use Virtual TV Keyboard to paste or type 10-digit UIDs and chat messages onto your TV effortlessly.
  • If code expired: Pairing codes expire after 30 minutes; press New Code on Fire TV.
  • Minimum Age & Privacy: Strictly 18+ adults only. Zero server message storage.
