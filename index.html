🔒 ZingTalk Enterprise Privacy Policy & Terms

Zero Server Storage Architecture Senior & Elderly Assistive Certified P2P Encrypted Audio/Video COPPA & GDPR Compliant
Effective Date: October 18, 2026 • Application: ZingTalk (com.zingtalk.com)

1. Purpose & Senior/Elderly Accessibility

ZingTalk is engineered as an accessible living-room smart TV communication platform specifically tailored for elderly parents, senior citizens, and families. Designed to bridge the digital divide, ZingTalk pairs physical TV remote D-Pad navigation with Amazon Alexa hands-free voice commands so older adults can easily call family members from their sofa without the complications of small smartphone screens, SIM cards, or complex password menus.

2. Zero Server Storage (Text, Voice, Video & Contacts)

  • Zero Cloud Database Storage for Chats: Messages are transmitted in real time and are never written to any database, disk, log file, or cloud backup.
  • Instant Memory Relay & Automatic Purge: If the recipient is temporarily offline, the encrypted payload is held temporarily in volatile server RAM solely until delivery. The millisecond the recipient receives the message, it is permanently and irreversibly purged from memory.
  • Direct Peer-to-Peer Media Streams: All audio and video calls operate via WebRTC DTLS-SRTP end-to-end encrypted pipelines directly between devices.
  • Contacts Directory (100% Local Device Storage): Saved contacts reside exclusively inside your local device memory (localStorage / local device preferences). Contacts are NEVER saved to Firebase Firestore, nor stored on any cloud database. Signing out permanently wipes your local contacts and chat records.

3. Anonymous 10-Digit Identity (No Phone or SIM Required)

ZingTalk replaces phone numbers, SIM card telemetry, and hardware IMEI identifiers with a randomized 10-digit numeric Unique Identifier (UID). You can connect and communicate freely without revealing personal phone numbers or identity.

4. Amazon Fire TV & Alexa Voice Privacy

  • Intent Signals Only: Voice commands issued via Amazon Alexa physical TV remotes or Echo devices transmit high-level action intents (e.g. "Call Rahul") via encrypted webhooks directly to your paired TV room.
  • No Voice Recording: ZingTalk never receives, processes, or archives raw microphone audio or spoken transcripts. Voice processing is handled exclusively on your local Alexa device.
  • Device Room Isolation: Each paired TV session is isolated to its unique 10-digit UID room, preventing cross-device interference.

5. Age Restriction & Audience Intent (Not for Children Under 13)

ZingTalk is designed and marketed exclusively for adults, senior citizens, and family caregivers. ZingTalk is NOT designed, marketed, or intended for use by unsupervised children under the age of 13. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and EU GDPR-K, ZingTalk does not knowingly harvest personal information from children under 13.

6. CRITICAL: No Emergency Services (911/112)

ZingTalk is an over-the-top internet calling tool and is NOT a traditional telephone replacement. ZingTalk CANNOT connect to emergency dispatchers (such as 911, 112, 999, or medical responders). Alternative landline or cellular telephone equipment must always be accessible in the household.

7. User Data Rights & Account Control

  • Right to Erasure: You can completely erase your local session, contacts, and chat cache at any time via the Sign Out button or device Settings.

📖 How to Use ZingTalk

🎮 Remote Control & D-Pad Navigation

• Arrow Keys (Up, Down, Left, Right): Navigate across buttons, dialpad, and contacts with high-contrast cyan highlight.
• Center OK / Select Button: Select or click any focused item.
• Back Button: Go back or close modals. During an active video/audio call, press Back twice to safely disconnect without accidental disconnects.

👆 Touchscreen & Mouse Support

• Works 100% with touchscreens, air-mouse, or standard mouse clicks on Smart TVs, tablets, and mobile devices.

📞 Making Calls with 10-Digit UID

• Every user has a unique 10-digit UID (shown at the top of the screen).
• Enter any contact's 10-digit UID in the Dialpad to start an instant HD Video or Audio call.
• Save frequent contacts in your TV address book for 1-click speed calling.

🔒 Privacy & Zero Server Retention

• All audio and video calls flow peer-to-peer (P2P) with WebRTC DTLS-SRTP encryption.
• No call recordings or private chats are ever saved to cloud disks.
