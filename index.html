Legal & Compliance

1. Privacy By Design (Zero Server Storage)

ZingTalk is built on strict data confidentiality. Photos, audio clips, voice recordings, and video clips shared between contacts are sent directly and stored ONLY in your local device memory.

We do NOT store your chat messages, pictures, voice notes, or videos on any central server or Firebase Cloud Storage.

2. Real-Time WebRTC Calling

Both Audio and Video calls utilize direct peer-to-peer WebRTC connections. Google STUN servers (stun.l.google.com) perform initial NAT routing only. Audio and video streams never pass through our servers and are never recorded.

3. Permissions Disclosure

  • Camera & Microphone: Required only during active live Video or Audio calls.
  • Storage: Required only to select images, audio, or video files you choose to share with contacts.

4. Developer Contact

For questions or support, contact zingarenaoffi1@gmail.com.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By installing or using ZingTalk (com.zingtalk.com), you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

2. Acceptable Conduct

Users must not transmit unlawful, defamatory, harassing, or abusive content. ZingTalk reserves the right to terminate access for abusive behavior.

3. Local Storage Responsibility

Because ZingTalk does not retain cloud backups of your messages or media files, users are responsible for device security and local data retention.
